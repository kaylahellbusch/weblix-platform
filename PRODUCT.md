# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Website managers working with local business clients. The public homepage should also earn a small business owner's trust.

## Product Purpose

Organize clients, review CSV imports, and save business details and website content together.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Existing homepage is the approved product truth. Search clients in grid or compact view. Add businesses manually or review and confirm CSV rows. Edit and save client details and website content. Rendering, live preview and publishing are not available yet. Preserve existing links, login behavior and navigation.

## Brand Commitments

Weblix name and existing vector logo. Approved palette and constraints in docs/design/ui-design-brief.md remain binding. No invented customer proof or capabilities.

## Evidence on Hand

Homepage synthetic examples use Juniper Flower Studio and Northline Plumbing. These are illustrations, not real customer evidence or interactive editors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Responsive desktop and mobile web, keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, 320px reflow and WCAG AA contrast.

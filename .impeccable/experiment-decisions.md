## Challenger judgments and retained discipline

Each fused challenger was weighed against the assigned guide on audience identification and product clarity, preserving the approved palette and true capabilities. None earned a full alternate:

- Sleeping city fold: folded client neighborhoods would obscure straightforward organization. Declined on both axes. Raise retained: make connected information visibly belong to one continuous record rather than three floating pieces.
- Mesophotic deep dive: a depth axis through details/import/content suggests a hierarchy the product does not have. Declined on both. Raise retained: a deliberate scroll sequence with a visible end, not repetitive panels.
- Kinetic sand glyphs: concentric client selection and relief controls introduce unfamiliar navigation. Declined on both. Raise retained: one coherent control vocabulary throughout.
- Du Bois data portraits: graphic arguments require quantitative evidence this homepage does not have. Declined on both. Raise retained: vary scale and density according to the actual content, rather than equal generic cards.
- WebGL shader portal: a living pixels portal offers no clearer evidence of client organization. Declined on both. Raise retained: a confident first-viewport focal move, without adding an unrelated shader.
- Rhythm machine step row: sequential playback invents timing and workflow states. Declined on both. Raise retained: consistent alignment and a legible real three-step sequence.

No challenger was seriously adopted or built; no catalog quality board exists for the grounded direction. No comp is claimed for the code-led build. No image generation or raster assets were necessary. No baseline was captured by explicit instruction.
